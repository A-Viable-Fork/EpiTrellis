# CAPTURE DYNAMICS TRELLIS

**Archetype:** Operational, Normative secondary
**Parent:** Epistemic Substrate Decomposition Trellis v1.4
**Hierarchy contract:** Trellis Radiation (sibling spawn)
**Sibling:** Receiver Substrate Trellis. Relationship type: **complementary**
**Domain:** Whether and under what conditions capability provision in a federated substrate remains contestable, given that every prior commons concentrated above an open waist
**Status:** Active. No gate sorry designated. Gate candidates listed; first Shore must select one or record why the trellis proceeds without.

---

> **Operational Intent.** This trellis governs the construction of diagnostic instruments and the theory they test: given a substrate where anyone may supply a capability, determine which capabilities concentrate anyway, by what mechanism, and what architectural response exists. The deliverable is checkable tests runnable against real systems, plus the capture theory those tests either support or break. What gets killed: any capture claim that reduces to interpretive judgment rather than a test; any architectural requirement that is a quality mandate in disguise; any exit route counted before footprint closure; any custody claim that survives every provider retaining a copy. What gets logged: every capability whose residue cannot be externalized, every apparent exit route that collapses under closure, every countermeasure whose deployment increases the probability of the attack. The trellis exists because the historical record is unanimous and unflattering: every commons that solved its abuse problem concentrated capability provision while its waist stayed open.

## JURISDICTIONAL BOUNDARY

This trellis addresses contestability of capability provision. It does not address:

- What must be built. That is the sibling's jurisdiction.
- The taxonomy of functions. That is the parent's.
- Competition, antitrust, or regulatory remedy. The instruments here describe structure; using them to argue for intervention is a different act with different standards.
- The merit or ranking of any competition submission, per the parent's boundary, which extends to every artifact in this chain.
- Whether concentration is bad. The trellis assumes irreversibility is the failure and dominance is not, per T0-EXOG, and that assumption is itself contestable elsewhere.

## CHILD-SPECIFIC TIER 0

**T0-CHECK. Capture claims must reduce to tests.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
Every claim that a capability is or is not contestable must reduce to a test runnable against a real system, returning a result a third party can reproduce. Interpretive criteria are the failure mode this tier exists to prevent: an earlier formulation turned on whether an asset was "conceptually separable" from the capability it served, and the hard case defeated it, because a spam corpus is generated by filtering rather than held alongside it.
*Falsification mode: a capture claim in this trellis cannot be stated as a procedure with a reproducible outcome.*

**T0-EXOG. Exogenous advantage is permitted.** `[Normative Commitment]`
Capital, compute, engineering quality, and brand are legitimate sources of provider advantage and are outside this trellis's targets. Two mechanisms are targets: endogenous learning advantage, where exercising a capability generates exclusive observations that improve it, and lock-in, where accumulated state, relations, or focality make displacement structurally impossible. Dominance is permitted. Irreversibility is the failure.
*Falsification mode: a proposed countermeasure constrains a provider's exogenous advantage, or a capture claim rests on a provider merely being better.*

**T0-CLOSURE. Route diversity is tested, never counted.** `[Structural Commitment]`
Apparent independence among exit routes, providers, or capability sources must pass footprint closure before being counted as independence. Closure is transitive over the rests-on relation, absorbs cycles per strongly connected component, and over-counts shared dependence, because the error that cannot be afforded is believing in an exit that does not exist.
*Falsification mode: an independence count is reported without closure, or closure is run in the anti-conservative direction.*

## CHILD-SPECIFIC TIER 1

**T1-LOOP. The concentration test.** `[Structural Commitment]`
A capability is endogenously self-concentrating when providing it generates observations that improve future provision and those observations remain unavailable to competitors. Both conjuncts are required: feedback alone does not concentrate if the learning residue is exported, and exclusivity alone does not concentrate without feedback. This is the parent's L7 at the market level rather than within one system's design, and the two are adjacent rules with a shared derivation rather than one rule applied twice.
*Falsification mode: a capability satisfying both conjuncts is exhibited that does not concentrate, or one failing either conjunct is exhibited that does.*

**T1-SCOPE. Residue locus follows scope.** `[Normative Commitment]`
Information generated by exercising a capability accrues at the narrowest non-provider locus whose scope matches what was learned. Public facts to the shared substrate, receiver-specific residue to the receiver, community-scoped residue to the community. Blanket publication is too coarse and destroys privacy where residue is receiver-specific.
*Falsification mode: a residue class is exhibited with no locus narrower than the provider, and no argument that the provider is the narrowest matching scope.*

**T1-COMP. Test against the comparative population first.** `[Empirical Commitment]`
Every capture claim is checked against the prior commons before being treated as novel: SMTP and sender reputation, chain governance and purchasable voting weight, ranking signals and their optimization industry, encyclopedic revert economics, platform distribution and identity gates. The pattern is regular: contribution cost collapses, abuse appears, receivers add defenses, defenses benefit from accumulated observation, capability provision concentrates, and the nominally open waist stays open while practical power centralizes above it.
*Falsification mode: a capture claim is advanced as new without the comparative check, or a prior case is cited for its mechanism rather than its structure.*

## LINTER

**[LINTER MODE: FLAG-PRIMARY WITH SELECTIVE KILL]** per Operational archetype. Tier 0 violations kill. Everything else flags with a redirect.

### L-B1. The Industrial Policy Slide `[KILL]`
*Test: has an architectural requirement become a mandate that competitors exist at comparable quality?*
Kill under T0-EXOG. The architecture may prevent a provider from obtaining advantage through mechanisms the substrate itself creates. It may not require that anyone be able to match a provider who is simply better resourced.

### L-B2. The Separability Dodge `[KILL]`
*Test: does a criterion turn on whether an asset is conceptually separable from the capability it serves?*
Kill under T0-CHECK. The hard case defeats it: the observation stream is generated by provision rather than held alongside it. Use T1-LOOP, which is checkable.

### L-B3. The Portable Copy `[FLAG]`
*Test: is custody claimed for residue while every provider that has processed a view retains it?*
Redirect: state what constrains retention after use. If nothing architectural does, record it as a legal or cryptographic dependency and price the cryptographic options, which are expensive. Portability without a retention constraint hands every provider a copy under the name of custody.

### L-B4. The Population Flywheel `[FLAG]`
*Test: is receiver-local custody being credited with removing a concentration loop whose advantage is cross-receiver?*
Redirect: separate individual from population flywheel. Custody removes the first. A model trained across millions of receivers holds advantage no single receiver's history reconstitutes, and that is the loop the comparative population actually demonstrates.

### L-B5. The Fake Exit `[KILL]`
*Test: are multiple routes counted as independent without closure?*
Kill under T0-CLOSURE. Worked instance: four nominal distribution routes over one identity predicate collapse to one.

### L-B6. The Advertised Defense `[FLAG]`
*Test: does a countermeasure function only by being legible to the adversary?*
Redirect: separate operation from announcement and decide each. Narrower than the general observation that protection attracts attack, which covers ordinary success and is nearly contentless. The tight class is a commitment problem: deterrence requires disclosure, disclosure is the trigger.

### L-B7. The Secrecy Residue `[FLAG]`
*Test: is residue externalization proposed for a capability whose residue is a specification for evasion?*
Redirect: adversarial residue is not non-rival, since part of its value is secrecy. Candidate partial answer is publishing outcomes rather than signals. Whether outcome-only residue degrades slowly enough to matter is empirical and unanswered.

## GATE CANDIDATES

No gate designated at spawn.

**GC-1. Game-integrity contestability.** Can capabilities whose residue is unpublishable be made contestable at all? Parent S-22. If no, the architecture's honest claim shrinks from preventing capture to preventing capture of everything except the defenses, which is smaller and still worth having. This is the strongest candidate: it is the place the comparative population is most unanimous and the place no answer exists.

**GC-2. Substitutability formulation.** Can capability-substitutable exit be stated as an architectural property without becoming a quality mandate? T1-LOOP is the current candidate and it has not been run against a real system.

**GC-3. Focality coordination.** Does the irreducible coordination half of fork-choice admit any mechanism that is not itself a capture surface, given that anything reliably producing convergence is more valuable than any other slot?

## EXCLUSION RESERVOIR

**AR-B01. State-survival as the anti-capture property**
- **Dead Entry:** Removing provider P may remove P's capabilities but must not invalidate unrelated objects, relations, identity, social graph, provenance, communities, or receiver state.
- **Target:** Stating architecturally what prevents provider capture.
- **Justifiable Intent:** State survival is necessary and the property is correct as far as it goes.
- **Killed By:** Email. Gmail satisfies it completely. Addresses stay portable, SMTP is unchanged, the corpus is not Gmail's, and Gmail remains load-bearing for whether mail is delivered, because deliverability quality has scale economies a small provider cannot buy past. The property protects state and is silent on whether an alternative is reachable at usable quality, which is what determines whether exit is real.
- **Block Scope:** Class Block on all state-only formulations of anti-capture.
- **Structural Residue:** State survival remains one of four exit conditions. B-4 carries the decomposition.
- **Linter constraint added:** L-B5 for the closure half; T1-LOOP for the substitutability half.
- **Reactivation Conditions:** A demonstration that capability substitutability is entailed by state survival in some restricted class of capabilities.
- **Date:** 2026-08-12

**AR-B02. Blanket residue publication**
- **Dead Entry:** Opening the concentration loop by requiring the learning residue of a capability to be published into the commons, so that provision enriches everyone rather than the incumbent.
- **Target:** Preventing endogenous concentration without preventing scale.
- **Justifiable Intent:** The inversion is right: do not stop positive feedback, open it. This is the strongest structural idea the program has produced against concentration.
- **Killed By:** Two cases. Receiver-specific residue cannot be published without destroying privacy, which is most personalization. Adversarial residue cannot be published because publication is an evasion specification, which is most of the game family. Blanket publication survives only for public facts, which are the capabilities least prone to concentration in the first place.
- **Block Scope:** Point Block. The mechanism survives, scoped.
- **Structural Residue:** T1-SCOPE. Residue accrues at the narrowest non-provider locus matching what was learned. Also the finding that the split falls the wrong way: the family that can externalize is the family that concentrates least.
- **Reactivation Conditions:** A privacy-preserving derived-observation format that carries learning value without carrying receiver identity, or an outcome-only format whose evasion degradation is measurably slow.
- **Date:** 2026-08-12

## SHORE PROTOCOL

**Cadence:** Event-driven.

**Trigger events:**
1. An instrument returns a result contradicting a stated capture claim.
2. A capability is identified whose residue has no locus narrower than the provider.
3. Closure collapses a route set previously counted as independent.
4. A comparative case is found whose structure breaks T1-COMP's stated pattern.
5. A sibling finding contradicts a commitment here.
6. A proposed countermeasure fires L-B6.

**Steps:**
1. Restate the failure without remedy.
2. Check against T0-CHECK: is the disputed claim a test or a judgment?
3. Check against T0-EXOG: does the repair constrain exogenous advantage?
4. Run T1-COMP: has this structure appeared in a prior commons, and did it resolve?
5. Amend, and record which of Tier 0, Tier 1, Linter, or Ledger moved.
6. Propagate to sibling and parent.
