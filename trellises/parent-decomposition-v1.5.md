# EPISTEMIC SUBSTRATE DECOMPOSITION TRELLIS

**Archetype:** Creative-Primary, Theory-building secondary
**Domain:** The minimum coordination substrate for federated epistemic commons, derived by decomposition of the FLF EpiStack competition field
**Status:** Active. Awaiting first seating-failure Shore.

---

> **Operational Intent.** This trellis governs the construction of a function decomposition: the complete set of jobs a federated epistemic commons performs, stated as jobs and never as mechanisms, sorted across four tiers (kernel, seam, periphery, participant) plus an ecosystem tier. The decomposition is built against an observed population rather than designed from first principles: twenty-two repositories submitted to or adjacent to the FLF EpiStack competition, plus the prior commons that solved narrower versions of the same problem. A decomposition that cannot seat a found submission is incomplete, and incompleteness is the trellis's central failure mode. Candidate functions and candidate tier placements are logged and redirected rather than killed, because the object under construction does not yet exist and premature rejection forecloses the space. What gets killed: any decomposition that fixes a vocabulary where the population varies, any construction that permits standing to transfer without local recomputation, any function statement that names a mechanism. What gets logged: every candidate function, every tier-placement disagreement across implementations, every slot the population leaves empty.

---

## JURISDICTIONAL BOUNDARY

This trellis addresses the decomposition of functions and their tier placement. It does not address:

- The merit or ranking of any competition submission. Descriptions of submissions are evidence about the design space and carry no assessment. Ranking is excluded from every artifact in this program's chain, not only from this document: a seating pass conducted with a ranking in context acquires a gradient, since once positions are ordered every cell has a stake. Where a competitive read is wanted, it is produced separately and never shares a context with a seating pass.
- The substantive questions in the three case studies. COVID origins, LHC safety, and egg nutrition appear here as corpus, never as claims to adjudicate.
- The internal development of the AVF EpiStack kernel. That kernel is one implementation among the population and enters as data.
- Funding, publication timing, and outreach sequencing. Those are operational decisions downstream of the decomposition.

These are legitimate questions. They require different infrastructure.

## STRATIFIED AXIOMATIC CORE

### Tier 0: Hard Vetoes

**T0-GN. Generative novelty as contamination burden.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
This is an archetype-level generator discipline, never a claim about what the domain contains. A true carving is not disqualified for resembling a prior one.

A carving substantially isomorphic to a member of the reference corpus (the FLF three-layer stack of ingestion, structure, and assessment; OSI and TCP/IP layering; Ostrom's eight design principles; Beer's Viable System Model; the AVF eleven-invariant register) carries a rebuttable presumption of contamination. Discharge requires showing which observed variation in the population forced the mapping, and that the derivation ran from the population rather than from the reference. Failure to discharge kills the carving. Structural concepts may be imported from any reference; unearned reproduction of one as a whole is the failure.
*Falsification mode: a bijection is exhibited between the register and a reference corpus member preserving tier placement, and no population-forced derivation is offered.*

**T0-GS. Global structure through legibility.** `[Structural Commitment]`
The carving must make cross-implementation variation visible. A function set read off one implementation at a time, then unioned, is a local construction and fails this tier. A carving that seats every submission while collapsing the differences between them also fails, at full coverage, because coverage without legible variation is the failure this tier exists to catch. Definition fixes a point to see how other points float; a carving that shows no floating has fixed nothing.
*Falsification mode: the decomposition is reconstructible from any single submission plus a union operation, with no cross-population structure entering; or two implementations known to differ on a design choice map to identical function-and-tier assignments with the difference unrecoverable from the assignment.*

**T0-FS. Function statement discipline.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
A function names a job and never a way of doing it. "Contamination detection" is a function. "Herfindahl index over the support set" is a contract. Any entry in the function tier that admits exactly one mechanism is a contract in the wrong tier.

The register does not contain functions. A function is found and virtual: a job the domain contains whether or not anyone names it, real without being actual, indifferent to which contract instantiates it. What the register contains is concepts, each a compression of a topology of contracts into a single citable point, made by an author who paid for the compression. The concept is the falsifiable object. The job it names is not.

Two error directions follow. A concept that admits exactly one mechanism has smuggled a made thing into a found slot. A concept whose compression drops something its topology contained produces a seating failure, which is why an unseatable submission indicts the carving rather than the submission.
*Falsification mode: a stated function is satisfiable by exactly one mechanism; or the statement contains a mechanism term; or a contract is exhibited that does the job while falling outside the concept.*

**T0-NL. No laundering.** `[Structural Commitment]`
No construction may permit standing, grade, confidence, or capability to transfer across a boundary without recomputation available on the receiving side. Transfer under a pinned shared bundle is recomputation. Transfer under an unpinned or unverifiable declaration is laundering.
*Falsification mode: a crossing path is exhibited in which the receiver's standing depends on the producer's assertion with no available recomputation.*

**T0-SEAT. Seating obligation.** `[Empirical Commitment]`
Every submission in the population must seat: each typed decision it makes corresponds to a function in the decomposition and a tier placement. A submission that cannot be seated indicates a missing function, not a deficient submission.
*Falsification mode: a submission is exhibited whose typed decisions have no corresponding function slot, and the trellis has not opened a candidate for it.*

### Tier 1: Mapping Principles

**T1-EX. Exercised over declared, for filling claims only.** `[Empirical Commitment]`
A submission fills a function when its artifact exercises the function. READMEs, specifications, and roadmaps are evidence about intent and never evidence about filling. Where the two disagree, the artifact governs. This rule governs the claim "X fills F" and governs nothing else.
*Falsification mode: a filling claim is traced to prose rather than to executable or inspectable artifact content.*

**T1-ARG. Functions enter on argument, never on count.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
Whether a job exists is independent of whether anyone has done it. A function admitted on argument rather than on population evidence carries four requirements: a named failure that occurs when the job goes undone, with its direction of error; a demonstration that no existing concept in the register covers it; a stated falsifier, which for adversarial functions means an attack path that stays cheap with the function filled; and an account of why the population does not exercise it. Opportunity limitation is a sufficient account. Absence of demand is a warning rather than an account.

Functions admitted this way are marked *derived* and never *seated*, so the register stays legible about which is which. Population count is asymmetric evidence: high variance across a dimension proves the dimension free, and low or zero count proves nothing in either direction, serving only as a prompt to ask why.
*Falsification mode: a derived function is entered without one of the four requirements, or is recorded as seated.*

**T1-TIER. Tier placement is free; the seam is not.** `[Structural Commitment]`
The same function appears in different tiers across implementations, and that variance is data rather than error. The existence of a boundary between the recomputable core and the generative periphery is invariant; where any given function sits relative to it is a contract.
Tier freedom is the default empirical hypothesis, not an axiom. A function may be shown structurally forced to one side of the seam, and such a showing amends this rule rather than violating it.
*Falsification mode: a function is exhibited that occupies the same tier in every population member, and no argument shows the placement is forced.*

**T1-EMPT. Emptiness is read, never declared.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
A function is empty for a given implementation when the implementation does not exercise it. Intent, deliberateness, and stated omission carry no weight, because they are producer assertions the receiver cannot verify.
*Falsification mode: an emptiness judgment in the decomposition is traced to an author's declaration rather than to artifact inspection.*

**T1-UNL. Filling unlocks; filling never admits.** `[Structural Commitment]`
Each function, when filled, is stated together with the capability its filling makes available. Functions carry no membership force. A statement of the form "an implementation must fill F to participate" belongs in a receiver's standard, never in the decomposition.
*Falsification mode: a function in the decomposition is stated as a requirement rather than as a precondition for a named capability.*

### Tier 2: Methodology

**T2-CONTAM. Convergence requires upstream check.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
Before counting independent arrivals at a design choice, check whether a shared upstream source instructed it. The FLF competition brief names specific desiderata, and implementations of a named desideratum are prompted rather than convergent. Convergence claims state which upstream sources were checked.
*Falsification mode: a convergence count is presented without a statement of the upstream sources checked against it.*

**T2-CITE. Every dimension claim cites an artifact.** `[Empirical Commitment]`
A claim that implementation X fills function F with contract C cites a path and a commit hash. Claims without a locator are candidates, marked as such.
*Falsification mode: a dimension table cell contains a value with no artifact locator.*

**T2-DIR. Direction of error stated per null.** `[Methodological Commitment]`
For each function, state whether leaving it empty removes a reading or inflates one. Deflationary nulls compose safely; inflationary nulls are the ones a receiver's standard must guard.
*Falsification mode: a function is entered in the decomposition with no direction-of-error annotation.*

## LINTER

**[LINTER MODE: INVERTED]** This trellis operates in flag-primary, redirect-secondary mode. No automatic rejection. Every failure produces a redirect instruction indicating how to modify the candidate rather than a verdict eliminating it.

**Generator mode: RAW.** The generator is authorized to produce formally weak, analogically stretched, partially justified candidate functions and tier placements. Rigor is the Linter's job. A candidate function with an unclear boundary is a better contribution than no candidate.

### L1. Unmapped vocabulary `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: does a candidate function use a term from a source domain without stating its structural correspondence to a job in the decomposition?*
Redirect: state the job the borrowed term denotes, in the decomposition's own vocabulary, then check whether an existing function already covers it.

### L2. False isomorphism `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: is a structural correspondence between the substrate and a prior commons asserted without decomposing both into functions and comparing them slot by slot?*
Redirect: decompose the source system into what each of its functions takes, returns, and guarantees. Compare at that level. An analogy that survives functional decomposition earns its place; one that does not becomes an Archive entry with its residue extracted.

### L3. Naked axioms `[FLAG MODE]`
*Test: is a constraint presented in kernel-internal vocabulary where the reader needs the job stated plainly?*
Flag: restate in job terms alongside the technical form.

### L4. The Hegemony Smuggle `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: does a proposed fixed element fix a vocabulary, a value set, or an enumeration, where the population varies?*
Redirect: separate the job from the vocabulary. Ask what breaks when the vocabulary is replaced while the job is still done. If nothing breaks, the vocabulary is a contract and the job is the candidate function.

### L5. The Certifier Posture `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: does the decomposition or its registry grade, rank, approve, or exclude an implementation?*
Redirect: convert the judgment into a description plus a recomputable check. "Fills F" becomes "declares F, hash H, verifiable by running O." Whether the declaration holds becomes a separate contestable claim that others may attach.

### L6. The Set Union Error `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: are capabilities being added across a composed path as though independent?*
Redirect: state each capability's preconditions on other capabilities, including preconditions on their quality rather than their presence. Resolve as a dependency graph. A weak precondition fails silently and is the dangerous case.

### L7. The Flow That Captures Flow `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: is a proposed signal measured in the same currency as the thing it allocates, such that producing the signal and gaming it are one operation?*
Redirect: check whether the signal circulates. A fold that transmits nothing back to its inputs has no cycle to ride. A signal inside its own loop requires confinement, damping, and an exit rather than a claim of immunity.

### L8. The Prompted Convergence `[FLAG MODE]`
*Test: is a count of independent arrivals being reported for a design choice that a shared brief named?*
Flag: separate convergence on the problem, which the brief may have supplied, from convergence on the solution shape, which it did not. Report both.

### L9. The Required-Tier Ratchet `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: has a precondition for a capability been restated as a condition of membership?*
Redirect: name the capability the precondition serves, and relocate the requirement to a receiver's standard where the party with the interest carries it.

### L10. The Local Read `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: was a candidate function derived by reading one implementation, with the population used only to confirm?*
Redirect: state what cross-population structure the function is responding to. A function that only one implementation motivates is a contract awaiting its function.

### L11. The Native Import `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: does a construct enter the Axiomatic Core, the function set, or a tier assignment carrying vocabulary or structure from another program (the ethics trellis, the lexicon, the architecture document, the Constitutional Kernel) without slot-by-slot functional decomposition?*
Redirect: route the construct to the Analogy Domain Library with a stated obligation naming what would falsify it in substrate terms. Inbound material grounds nothing until re-derived locally. Familiarity is not derivation: material from the author's own prior programs receives the same treatment as material from a foreign domain, and receives it more strictly, because shared authorship makes the import feel native when it has not been earned.

### L12. The Premature Compile `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: is a function, format field, or capability moving into a build artifact while still a candidate, lacking a named capability, a direction-of-error annotation, or at least one artifact locator showing something exercises it?*
Redirect: hold it in the Discharge Ledger as a sorry. A candidate in the trellis is cheap to kill. The same candidate compiled into an entry format that others adopt becomes unkillable by adoption rather than by being right.

### L13. The Neighbor Seat `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: is one exercised artifact behavior credited to two or more adjacent concepts without a distinct job-specific witness for each?*
Redirect: state each concept's null failure and name the artifact behavior that prevents it. If one behavior prevents only one null failure, remove the other seat. If it genuinely does two jobs, record two witness statements.

This rule is F07 applied to the register itself. One behavior generating several seats is correlated evidence counted as independent, one level up, which is the failure the population spent twenty-one implementations learning to detect. Observed instances: source identity credited as semantic grounding, producer confidence credited as receiver-local standing, crux ranking credited as sensitivity, dependence detection credited again as generic pathology, preserved history credited as withdrawal propagation.

### L14. The Repo Boundary `[REDIRECT MODE]`
*Test: has the normalization unit been equated with one repository where the implementation declares multiple repositories, components, or clients as one exercised system; or has a multi-artifact workspace been treated as monolithic where only a subcomponent is exercised?*
Redirect: declare the implementation boundary first, then seat component-level witnesses inside it. Include an external artifact when the implementation explicitly identifies it as an exercised component, prototype, pilot, or deployment. Do not inherit capability from generic dependencies, prior art, or downstream projects merely because they are linked.

Boundary correction must run in both directions and must run symmetrically across the population. A widening that only ever adds seats is a coverage device rather than a fidelity rule. A widening applied to one implementation obliges the same pass on every implementation whose manifest declares components, and the author's own entry is the one where the sharpening half must be applied first.

**Linter evolution via post-mortem.** Linter rules are retrospectively generated from post-mortems of significant failures. After resolving a major crisis, ask: what structural blindness allowed this error to persist? Can we encode a check that would have caught it at the gate? Each class-block kill in the Exclusion Reservoir is a candidate for a new Linter rule.

**Constraint accumulation.** Each class-block entry in the Exclusion Reservoir adds a Linter constraint narrowing the generator's output space. Record the added constraint in the Archive entry.

## EXCLUSION RESERVOIR (ARCHIVE OF CONDITIONAL VIABILITY)

Two anticipated entries seeded from prior work. First live entries populated after the first seating failure.

**AR-01. The eleven-invariant required tier** `Anticipated, attempted in prior program`
- **Dead Entry:** A fixed tier of eleven invariants stated as conditions any kernel must satisfy to participate in composition.
- **Target Domain / Target Sorry:** Composition without laundering across independently built kernels.
- **Justifiable Intent:** Shared invariants do enable recomputation across a boundary. The invariants themselves survive.
- **Killed By:** T0-FS and observed population variance. A dimension filled five ways by five implementations admits no shared requirement. The invariants were preconditions for specific capabilities, misfiled as admission criteria.
- **Block Scope:** Class Block. Blocks the whole family of membership-conditioned protocol designs.
- **Structural Residue:** The eleven statements survive as capability preconditions. The untyped floor survives as the general mechanism. Content-addressed pinning survives as the switch between native and floored crossing.
- **Linter constraint added:** L9.
- **Reactivation Conditions:** A demonstration that some capability's precondition set is universal across all uses of the substrate, such that no receiver could rationally decline it.
- **Trellis Version at Block:** v1.0
- **Date:** 2026-08-11

**AR-02. Demotion-oracle-first method** `Anticipated, not yet attempted`
- **Dead Entry:** Establishing the fixed tier by exhibiting, for each candidate invariant, a concrete laundering or non-recomputation between two otherwise-conforming kernels when it is dropped.
- **Target Domain / Target Sorry:** Deciding which functions belong in the recomputable tier.
- **Justifiable Intent:** An existence proof is stronger evidence than a count.
- **Killed By:** Cost ordering. The method requires an existence proof per candidate before any cataloguing begins, where counting the population settles the free tier by observation. Also conflicts with the coordination layer's own position that fitness is revealed in use rather than certified up front.
- **Block Scope:** Point Block. The method is deferred rather than refuted.
- **Structural Residue:** The oracle remains the right instrument for the short list of non-varying dimensions, applied after the count narrows the field.
- **Reactivation Conditions:** The population count returns a candidate fixed tier of three or fewer dimensions.
- **Trellis Version at Block:** v1.0
- **Date:** 2026-08-11

## SHORE PROTOCOL

**Cadence:** Event-driven. No calendar trigger.

**Trigger events:**
1. A submission or artifact is found whose typed decisions do not seat in the current function set.
2. A function slot is identified that no population member exercises.
3. A function is observed in three or more different tier placements across implementations.
4. A candidate function is proposed that an existing function already covers, indicating a boundary error rather than a gap.
5. An analogy from the Analogy Domain Library survives functional decomposition and yields a function not already present.
6. A Linter rule fires three times on distinct candidates, indicating the rule is doing structural work and may belong in a tier.

**Shore steps, in order:**
1. Restate the failure without remedy.
2. Check whether the failure is a missing function, a misplaced tier, or a boundary drawn wrong between two existing functions.
3. Check the failure against T0-GN: does the repair move the decomposition toward a reference corpus member?
4. Check the failure against T0-GS: is the repair motivated by one implementation or by cross-population structure?
5. Amend the function set, the tier assignment, or the Linter. Record which.
6. Propagate: state which Discharge Ledger entries the amendment touches.

### Ontological Shore

Triggered when the decomposition's own categories are in question rather than its contents.

**Trigger events:**
1. The four-tier sort (kernel, seam, periphery, participant) fails to place a function, or places it arbitrarily.
2. A function is shown to be two jobs fused, or two functions shown to be one job split.
3. The distinction between function, contract, and standard fails on a case.
4. The population exercises something that is neither a function of the substrate nor a contract filling one.

**Ontological Shore steps:**
1. State the category that failed and the case that broke it.
2. Ask whether the category was imported from a reference corpus member without earning its place here.
3. Generate at least two alternative sortings before selecting one.
4. Check each alternative against the full population, not against the breaking case alone.
5. Record the rejected alternatives in the Archive with their residue.

## LEAKAGE PROTOCOL

Two gates, both instances of the untyped floor applied one level up. A construct arriving from elsewhere participates and grounds nothing until a local act re-derives it.

**Inbound.** Material from the ethics trellis, the philosophical lexicon, the architecture document, the Constitutional Kernel, and any prior commons case enters the Analogy Domain Library. Entry to a tier or to the function set requires passage through L2 and L11: both sides decomposed into what each function takes, returns, and guarantees, compared slot by slot, with the transferred content stated independent of the source vocabulary. Every library entry carries an obligation naming what would falsify it in substrate terms.

Author-shared sources receive stricter treatment than foreign ones. Shared authorship, shared method, and shared tooling make a construct feel derived when it has been inherited. Under T2-CONTAM, two documents by the same author using the same method are one source, and agreement between them is transfer rather than convergence.

**Outbound.** A function reaches a build artifact carrying three things: the capability its filling unlocks, its direction-of-error annotation per T2-DIR, and at least one artifact locator per T2-CITE. Absent any of the three it stays in the Discharge Ledger. The asymmetry is deliberate: a candidate held in the trellis is killed at the cost of an Archive entry, and the same candidate shipped in an entry format that others adopt is thereafter defended by its adopters rather than by its evidence.

**Standing outbound holds.** No entry format ships with capability-unlock as a structural commitment while S-13 is open.
